# Code of Conduct

RepoHealth aims to provide a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free environment for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

This policy is adapted from the principles of [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in discussions and reviews.
- Give and receive feedback graciously, and focus criticism on ideas rather than people.
- Respect differing viewpoints and lived experiences.
- Help keep project spaces welcoming and focused on the work.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, unwelcome sexual attention, and publishing another person's private information without permission are not acceptable. Disruptive conduct that prevents respectful project participation is also not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this policy. They may remove or edit contributions that violate it and may take proportionate action, including a warning, temporary restriction, or exclusion from project spaces.
